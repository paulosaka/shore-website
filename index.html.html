About SIERL

Integrated Oil & Gas Services Across Nigeria & West Africa

SIERL is an integrated oil and gas services company providing comprehensive logistics, procurement, marine support, petroleum supply, and technical services to upstream and downstream operators across Nigeria and West Africa.

We are strategically positioned to support exploration, drilling, production, and infrastructure operations through a seamless combination of oilfield logistics, marine support, fuel supply, and technical services. Our integrated approach ensures operational continuity, safety excellence, and regulatory compliance.

With deep industry expertise and a commitment to best practices, SIERL enables operators to maintain uninterrupted operations in both onshore and offshore environments while adhering to the highest standards of quality and safety.

Market Growth Drivers

  • Petroleum Industry Act (PIA): Creating opportunities for indigenous service providers
  • Dangote Refinery Impact: 650,000 bpd capacity reducing import dependence
  • IOC Divestments: Rise of local operators creating demand for integrated services
  • Gas Infrastructure: 209 Tcf gas reserves driving midstream investments

SIERL Positioning: As indigenous operators acquire divested assets, demand for integrated service providers like SIERL is growing rapidly. Our comprehensive service portfolio positions us as a strategic partner for this transformation.
